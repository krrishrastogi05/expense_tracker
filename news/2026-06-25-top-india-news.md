# Top 3 India News Stories — 25 June 2026

1. **Under-construction warehouse collapses in Kolkata's Taratala, killing five**
   *Source: The Statesman / Indiablooms*
   The iron roof of an under-construction tea warehouse in Kolkata's Taratala area collapsed on 24 June, killing at least five workers and trapping several others under concrete slabs and twisted iron beams. West Bengal Chief Minister Mamata Banerjee said an initial assessment found the building's design plan was faulty, and rescue operations were continuing.

2. **Mahua Moitra accuses BJP of engineering TMC "rebellion" amid party rift**
   *Source: The Federal*
   TMC Lok Sabha MP Mahua Moitra dismissed the wave of defections from her party as a BJP-orchestrated "operation" rather than genuine rebellion, insisting the party still belongs to Mamata Banerjee. She also drew attention for praising BJP's Suvendu Adhikari, fueling further speculation about internal TMC tensions.

3. **Padma Shri awardee Anil Kumar Rastogi clarifies viral "snub" of PM Modi**
   *Source: Free Press Journal*
   83-year-old actor and Padma Shri recipient Anil Kumar Rastogi addressed a viral clip from the Padma Awards ceremony showing him appearing to walk past PM Modi's extended hand. Rastogi said the moment was an unintentional oversight due to his age, not a deliberate snub, and reaffirmed his respect for the Prime Minister.
